# UDST policy snapshot

Accessed 16 July 2026. The application links users to the source pages and labels its output unofficial.

- GPA is a credit-weighted average on a 0.00–4.00 scale: [Final Grade Procedure](https://www.udst.edu.qa/about-udst/institutional-excellence-ie/policies-and-procedures/final-grade-procedure).
- Only the highest attempt is included for a repeated course; undergraduate repeats are limited to D+ or lower and a course may be repeated twice: [Final Grade Policy](https://www.udst.edu.qa/about-udst/institutional-excellence-ie/policies-and-procedures/final-grade-policy).
- W, AU, EN, and other non-calculable final grades do not enter CGPA; transfer credit is recorded as EN without GPA impact: [Final Grade Procedure](https://www.udst.edu.qa/about-udst/institutional-excellence-ie/policies-and-procedures/final-grade-procedure) and [Transfer Policy](https://www.udst.edu.qa/about-udst/institutional-excellence-ie/policies-and-procedures/transfer-policy).
- Undergraduate clear standing begins at a 2.00 CGPA. Health clinical progression and work-term eligibility have additional conditions: [Academic Standing Policy](https://www.udst.edu.qa/about-udst/institutional-excellence-ie/policies-and-procedures/academic-standing-policy).
- Graduation CGPA uses courses aligned to the approved graduation program, excludes exemptions, and applies the highest repeat grade: [Graduation Requirements](https://www.udst.edu.qa/current-student/graduation-requirements).
- Prerequisites and corequisites come from published program plans: [All Programs](https://www.udst.edu.qa/admissions/all-programs) and [Academic Advising](https://www.udst.edu.qa/life-udst/student-central-services/student-central-servicesmain/student-central-services/academic-0).
